## How to test the idea properly

1. Confirm event year, first railway impact time, asset ID/coordinates and relevant upstream flow path.
2. Recover original public resident posts with publication time, event time, location confidence and source URL. Preserve unresolved evidence rather than inferring timestamps from search results or repost dates.
3. Delineate asset catchments; account for prairie depressions, storage and event-dependent drainage connectivity.
4. Replay weather/SWE, gauges and posts using their **available-at-the-time** timestamps, with comparable quiet days.
5. Compare baseline weather/gauges versus the added social layer for lead time, missed events and false positives.
